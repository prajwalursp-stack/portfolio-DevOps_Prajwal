About Me

Engineering Resilient, Low-Friction Delivery

I am a DevOps Engineer based in Bengaluru, India, with a deep dedication to continuous automation, site reliability, and bridging the gap between developers and operations. Having started with strong software quality foundations, I transitioned into core DevOps and Cloud Infrastructure, bringing a test-first, shift-left mindset to modern delivery pipelines.

Over the past 4.8 years, I have architected and scaled end-to-end continuous delivery ecosystems for enterprise clients like Claritev Healthcare Technologies through Quality-AI. My day-to-day focus centers on eliminating deployment friction: creating standardized Jenkins Groovy shared libraries, setting up one-click Kubernetes manifest releases, and engineering instant auto-rollback mechanisms that guarantee 99.9% release safety.

Core Engineering Philosophy: "Every manual step is an incident waiting to happen. Build pipelines that treat infrastructure as reproducible code, shift security and test validation left, and make production deployments boring and fail-safe."
